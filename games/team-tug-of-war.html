How to Play

Team Tug of War

Goal: your team drags the knot into its zone.

  1. Three teams pull one shared rope.
  2. Every pull adds force to your team's side.
  3. The knot moves toward whoever pulls hardest.
  4. Own 80% of the pull, or lead at time, to win.
ControlsClick PULL or mash Space
