How to Play

Quick Draw Guess

Goal: draw clearly, and guess fast.

  1. On your turn, draw the secret word — the room guesses it.
  2. You score for every player who guesses your drawing.
  3. On other turns, a drawing appears — pick the right word.
  4. Faster correct guesses score more.
ControlsMouse drag to draw · click to guess
