How to Play

Color Wars

Goal: your team paints the most tiles.

  1. Click any tile to fill its claim meter.
  2. It flips to your team's color when the meter fills.
  3. Clicking a rival's tile drains it, then steals it.
  4. Most tiles when the timer ends wins the round.
ControlsMouse click — fast, and re-click to fill faster
ScoringWinning team +8 · +1 per tile you flip
