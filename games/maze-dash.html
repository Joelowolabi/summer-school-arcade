How to Play

Maze Dash

Goal: reach the exit first.

  1. You start at the corner; the exit glows green.
  2. Steer through the maze to reach it.
  3. Everyone races the same maze at once.
  4. Faster finishes score more — best of 3 mazes.
ControlsWASD or arrow keys
ScoringPlacement points each maze · 3 mazes
