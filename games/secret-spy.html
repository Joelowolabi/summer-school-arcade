How to Play

Secret Spy

Goal: find the Spy — or survive as the Spy.

  1. Your squad of 5 gets a secret theme — except the Spy.
  2. In turn, each player gives one clue word that fits.
  3. The Spy has to fake a clue and blend in.
  4. Everyone votes on who the Spy is.
