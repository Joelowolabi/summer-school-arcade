How to Play

Build-a-Monster

Goal: build the funniest, scariest or weirdest monster.

  1. You're assigned one body part — draw it blind.
  2. Five parts stitch into one composite monster.
  3. All 30 players vote in three categories.
  4. One monster can win more than one category.
