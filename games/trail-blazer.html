How to Play

Trail Blazer

Goal: be the last rider blazing.

  1. Your bike leaves a glowing wall behind it.
  2. Turn with the keys to steer — you can't reverse.
  3. Crash into any wall (yours or theirs) and you're out.
  4. Last rider wins; top survivors reach the Finale.
ControlsWASD or arrow keys
