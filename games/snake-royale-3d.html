Snake Royale

Goal: be the last snake slithering.

  1. Steer your snake around the 3D arena.
  2. Eat glowing pellets to grow longer.
  3. Hitting a wall, yourself or another snake ends your run.
  4. Top survivors of each heat reach the Finale.
