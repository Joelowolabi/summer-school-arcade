How to Play

Doodle Duel

Goal: draw the secret word best.

  1. Your team gets a secret word — draw it in 60 seconds.
  2. Your team votes for its favorite (not your own).
  3. Each team's winner enters the Finale Gallery.
  4. All 30 players vote for the champion doodle.
