How to Play

Word Rain

Goal: zap words before they reach the line.

  1. Words fall from the top of the screen.
  2. Type a word exactly to zap it away.
  3. Each word that crosses the line costs a life.
  4. It speeds up — score as much as you can before time or lives run out.
